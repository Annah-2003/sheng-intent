## Material limitations

Some reviewed fragments depend on the prompt or have debatable intent boundaries. The final errors include a reminder labelled list addition and a recommendation labelled playback. Review status does not establish a perfect gold standard. The sample is small, convenience-based, and scenario-grouped rather than speaker-held-out. Cross-scenario semantic similarity remains possible. A documented pilot, second-reviewer agreement study and formal usability/access study are not available.

No training setting, label or split has been altered in response to final-test errors. A revised annotation experiment would require a separate declared evaluation plan. See RESULTS.md for actual errors. The local deliverables folder contains the internal rubric assessment; it is not an organiser score.
